About DietMe

DietMe is an AI-powered preventive metabolic health platform designed specifically for adults 35 and older. We exist because the gap between lifestyle choices and metabolic consequences is invisible until symptoms appear.

Most health apps focus on calorie counting or fitness tracking. DietMe focuses on the metabolic patterns that determine whether your body is building stability or accumulating risk. Our 25-question assessment evaluates five critical domains: body composition, glucose stability, liver load, inflammation, and activity resilience.

The result is a clear, interpretable metabolic stability score with personalized directives and structured 4-week protocols that target your weakest domain first.

Our Approach

We use rule-based scoring algorithms informed by published research in metabolic health, insulin resistance, hepatic fat metabolism, systemic inflammation, and exercise physiology. Our language is deliberately non-diagnostic: we identify patterns, not diseases.

Our Science

DietMe's scoring methodology is grounded in established metabolic health research. Our five domains reflect the major modifiable pillars of metabolic stability:

Body Composition

Waist-to-height ratio is used as the primary indicator, supported by research showing it outperforms BMI for predicting cardiometabolic risk. Abdominal adiposity is the strongest modifiable predictor of metabolic syndrome.

Glucose Stability

Post-meal energy patterns, cravings, and hypoglycemic symptoms serve as proxy indicators for glycemic variability. Research consistently links glucose instability to insulin resistance progression.

Liver Load Pattern

Dietary processing burden (ultra-processed foods, sugary beverages, alcohol) drives hepatic fat accumulation. Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease affects an estimated 25% of the global population.

Inflammation & Recovery

Sleep quality, stress, and cognitive symptoms (brain fog) correlate with systemic inflammatory markers including CRP, IL-6, and TNF-alpha. Sleep consistency may matter more than duration.

Activity Resilience

Non-exercise activity thermogenesis (NEAT) and resistance training preserve insulin sensitivity and metabolically active tissue. Research supports 150+ minutes of moderate activity and 2+ resistance sessions weekly.
