Evidence-Based Foundation

Our system synthesizes official Food-Based Dietary Guidelines (FBDGs) from 100+ countries, combined with clinical evidence on portion control and sustainable weight loss.

Country-Specific

Links to official FBDGs from FAO/WHO repositories. Adapts local ingredients, meal patterns, and measurement units while maintaining universal plate geometry principles.

Safety First

Medical restrictions (celiac, allergies, diabetes) trigger hard constraints and cross-contact warnings. Gradual weight loss pace (0.5-1kg/week) as recommended by CDC/WHO.

Portion Geometry

Uses validated plate method (½ veg, ¼ protein, ¼ starch) rather than rigid diet names. Standardized serving equivalents allow precise portion control across any cuisine.

Important Safety Information

Medical Restrictions Are Not Preferences

If you have selected Celiac Disease, food allergies, kidney disease, or diabetes, please note:

  • This tool provides general guidance only and does not replace medical advice
  • Always verify ingredient labels for allergens, even with "safe" recipes
  • Cross-contact warnings are provided but cannot guarantee safety in home kitchens
  • Diabetes meal planning should be verified with your healthcare provider

Weight Loss Expectations

Research indicates gradual weight loss (1-2 lbs or 0.5-1kg per week) is more sustainable than rapid loss. Our calculator defaults to safe, conservative deficits.

Children & Special Populations

This tool is designed for adults 18+. Pregnant individuals, children, or those with eating disorder histories should seek specialized medical nutrition therapy.
